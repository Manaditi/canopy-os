## 🛠️ Recording Setup & Pro Tips

1. **Recommended Free Screen Recorders:**
   - **Loom (Browser extension / Desktop):** Free, records screen + small webcam bubble in the corner.
   - **OBS Studio:** High-definition 1080p 60fps recording.
   - **Windows Game Bar (`Win + G`):** Built into Windows, press `Win + Alt + R` to start/stop recording immediately.
   - **Clipchamp:** Built into Windows 11 for quick trims and audio volume boost.

2. **Browser Preparation Checklist:**
   - [ ] Open [https://manaditi.github.io/canopy-os/](https://manaditi.github.io/canopy-os/) in Chrome/Edge.
   - [ ] Press `F11` for clean full-screen presentation (hides bookmarks and taskbar).
   - [ ] Ensure browser zoom is at **100%** (`Ctrl + 0`).
   - [ ] Close notifications and messaging apps to avoid popups.

3. **Audio Quality:**
   - Speak in a steady, confident, energetic tone.
   - Keep microphone ~15–20 cm from your mouth.
